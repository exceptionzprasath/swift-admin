Complete technical specification, data flow, mathematical formulas, and cross-module integrations.

1. Overview & Core Mission

The CreatonsHR Payroll Engine (accessible via /admin/payroll) automates end-to-end Indian compensation and statutory disbursements. It eliminates spreadsheet inaccuracies by continuously syncing with Biometric Time & Attendance, Shift Rosters, Leave Calendars, Overtime Approvals, and Salary Revisions.

Statutory Compliance Guarantee: Conforms strictly to the EPF Act 1952 (₹15,000 ceiling, ₹1,800 standard cap, EPS 8.33%), ESIC Act 1948 (₹21,000 gross barrier, 0.75% / 3.25%), State-specific Professional Tax (PT), Labour Welfare Fund (LWF), and Indian Income Tax TDS regimes.

5. Core Mathematical Formulas

Proration Factor

Payable Days = Present + (0.5 × HalfDays) + Leaves + ApprovedRestDays

Proration Factor (P) = Payable Days ÷ Working Days per Month

Hourly Overtime Rate

Hourly Rate = Fixed Monthly Gross ÷ (Working Days × Working Hours)

OT Pay = Hourly Rate × Approved OT Hours × Multiplier (2.0×)

Provident Fund (EPF)

Base: Earned Basic + DA (Wage Ceiling: ₹15,000)

• If Base ≥ ₹15,000: Employee PF = ₹1,800 standard cap
• If Base < ₹15,000: Employee PF = Base × 12%
• Employer PF = Base × 12% (EPS 8.33% max ₹1,250 + EPF 3.67%)

Employees' State Insurance (ESIC)

Eligibility Barrier: Fixed Monthly Gross ≤ ₹21,000

• If Gross ≤ ₹21,000: Employee ESI = Earned Gross × 0.75%
• Employer ESI = Earned Gross × 3.25%
• If Gross > ₹21,000: Exempt (₹0)

Net Take-Home Salary:
Net Pay = Total Gross Earnings − (Emp PF + Emp ESI + PT + TDS + LWF + Loan EMI + Advances + Fines)

6. Real-World Case Study Walkthrough

Scenario: Associate with 1 Sunday Worked and 12 Hours Overtime.

  • Fixed Monthly Gross: ₹20,000 | Working Days: 26
  • Attendance Record: 24 Days Present, 1 Sunday Approved Work (8h), 12 Hours Overtime Approved.
  • Payable Days: 24 + 1 = 25 Days. Proration: 25 / 26 = 0.9615.
  • Hourly Rate: ₹20,000 / (26 × 8) = ₹96.15 / hour.
  • Overtime Earning (2×): 12h × (₹96.15 × 2) = ₹2,308.
  • Prorated Fixed Gross: ₹20,000 × (25 / 26) = ₹19,231.
  • Total Gross Pay: ₹19,231 + ₹2,308 (OT) = ₹21,539.
  • Deductions:
    • Fixed Gross is ₹20,000 (≤ ₹21,000), so ESI applies: ₹19,231 × 0.75% = ₹144.
    • Earned Basic (50%): ₹9,615. Employee PF (12%): ₹1,154.
    • Professional Tax (PT): ₹200.
    • Total Deductions = ₹1,154 + ₹144 + ₹200 = ₹1,498.
  • Net Salary Transferred to Bank: ₹21,539 − ₹1,498 = ₹20,041.
