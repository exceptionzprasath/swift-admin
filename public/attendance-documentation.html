Attendance Engine Architecture & Ingestion Flow

Complete technical specification, ADMS push protocol, punctuality evaluation, rest day work guard, and cross-module synchronization.

1. Overview & Core Mission

The CreatonsHR Attendance Engine (accessible via /admin/attendance) ingests punches from physical biometric hardware (fingerprint, face, RFID), touchless tablet kiosks, mobile GPS geofenced check-ins, and administrative regularizations. It pairs punches into daily check-in / check-out records, computes worked durations, flags punctuality, and syncs seamlessly with payroll.

Hardware Push Standard: Native support for ZKTeco, eSSL, Biomax, and Realtime biometric terminals via the HTTP/HTTPS ADMS protocol (/iclock/cdata), eliminating manual USB thumb drive data exports.

5. Punctuality Evaluation Engine

The punctuality status of each punch is evaluated dynamically against shift schedules:

Status Badge Condition / Evaluation Trigger Payroll Impact
On Time Check-In $\le$ Shift Start + Grace Minutes (e.g. $\le$ 09:15 AM for 09:00 AM shift) 1.0 Paid Day
Grace Window Check-In within allowed grace threshold (e.g. 09:01 AM to 09:15 AM) 1.0 Paid Day
Late (Xm) Check-In exceeded grace window. Displays exact delay (e.g. Late (24m)) 1.0 Paid Day (subject to 3-late-arrival penalty rules)
Half Day Worked 4.0h to 6.0h, or checked in past half-day cutoff time 0.5 Paid Day
Absent Scheduled working day with zero punches recorded 0.0 Paid Day (triggers Loss of Pay)
Approved Leave Sanctioned leave in Requests module (CL, SL, EL) 1.0 Paid Day (deducted from leave bank)
Rest Day Work (Pending) Employee punched on Sunday or Holiday; awaiting admin approval Zero credit until approved
Rest Day Work (Approved) Admin clicked "Approve Sunday Work" Credited to Extra Paid Days & Overtime Pay

6. Sunday & Public Holiday Rest Day Work Workflow

Punches recorded on non-working days are automatically isolated in an approval queue to prevent unverified salary inflation:

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> Ingested: Punch logged on Sunday / Public Holiday
    Ingested --> PendingApproval: Auto-flagged by Attendance Engine
    note right of PendingApproval: Badge: Yellow\n"Rest Day Work (Pending Approval)"\nor "Holiday Work (Pending Approval)"
    
    PendingApproval --> Approved: Admin clicks "Approve Sunday/Holiday Work"
    note right of Approved: Badge: Green\n"Rest Day Work (Approved · 8h)"\nHours credited to Payroll & Extra Days
    
    PendingApproval --> Declined: Admin clicks "Decline Sunday/Holiday Work"
    note right of Declined: Badge: Gray\n"Rest Day Work (Declined)"\nPunch retained for audit; 0 payroll credit
    
    Approved --> PayrollSync: Synced to computeMonthlyAttendanceSummary()
    Declined --> PayrollIgnored: Excluded from payable days
1-Click Approval Action: In /admin/attendance, click the Quick... dropdown on the employee's row and select "Approve Sunday Work". The badge immediately turns green and the hours are unlocked for month-end payroll.
